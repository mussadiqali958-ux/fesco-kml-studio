TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

Your data stays yours.

CSV parsing and KML generation happen in your browser. This application does not upload survey files to an application server.

  • Local processing — files are read by your browser session.
  • No account required — no sign-in or cloud storage.
  • Direct exports — generated files download to your device.

Map previews request OpenStreetMap tiles when enabled. External map tiles are not part of your uploaded CSV.
